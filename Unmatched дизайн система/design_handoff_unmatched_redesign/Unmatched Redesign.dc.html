3Match rows that survive every game mode

The domain has four modes — OneVsOne, TeamVsTeam, FreeForAll, Cooperative — and a fighter carries Team, Placement, HpLeft, CardsLeft, ActionsMade, Turn, plus an optional Villain with its Minions. So a "Game mode" column can't carry it. The fix in all three options is the same idea: one participants column that renders sides, not columns — a side is a box of fighter chips, sides are joined by VS (or ranked by placement in FFA), and the villain's minions hang off the villain chip. The row shape then tells you the mode at a glance and the badge only confirms it. Every option below shows the same four real cases: 1v1, 2v2, free-for-all, co-op vs villain.
