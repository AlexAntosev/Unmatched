14Match sheet — vertical participant cards, Ranked instead of Tournament, drag-to-reorder turn

The match sheet — one component (MatchSheetCards) that both Add match and the last step of the draft mount. No Tournament or Stage (they come from the bracket) — that slot holds Ranked / Casual instead. The participant plate is a 186px vertical card: hero key art on top (118px, the turn badge or the FFA medal riding its corner, grip in the other corner), the hero dropdown right under the art, then the player dropdown, then HP left / Cards / Sidekick stacked as full-width steppers. Every control is now the same width, so labels never truncate to “A.” the way they did when the plate was horizontal and squeezed two-per-side. Cards wrap centred inside their side box, which is what lets four of them sit in a row in FFA and two per team in 2v2 without the sheet growing wider. All four modes are live — switch them in the header.

14aMatch sheet — 1v1 / 2v2 / FFA / Co-op, switchable

Step 5 of 12b is this same component with prefilled on. Turn order: drag a card by the handle at the top of its art — 1v1 and FFA renumber 1-2-3 left to right, 2v2 alternates teams (1-3 / 2-4), co-op has no turn order at all.

12Add match, two ways — the entry modal and a working draft session

Add match now asks one question first: are you logging a finished game or setting one up? 12a is that modal — Manual entry goes straight to 4b, Draft session opens 12b. “Quiz” reads like a questionnaire, so the flow is called a draft — that is what it is: ban and pick, the vocabulary players already use. 12b is live, click through the whole thing: pick a mode and players (2v2 can split teams by hand or roll them), ban maps one player each from a pool of N+1, ban and pick from N×3 heroes in a random order (snake: forward, then back), cross one of your two in the final phase, roll turn order (alternating teams in 2v2, a villain step instead in co-op), and land on the prefilled sheet with fighters already sorted by turn. Every random pool has a Reroll next to it. Heroes and maps without art in the repo fall back to Unknown.png.

3Match rows that survive every game mode

The domain has four modes — OneVsOne, TeamVsTeam, FreeForAll, Cooperative — and a fighter carries Team, Placement, HpLeft, CardsLeft, ActionsMade, Turn, plus an optional Villain with its Minions. So a "Game mode" column can't carry it. The fix in all three options is the same idea: one participants column that renders sides, not columns — a side is a box of fighter chips, sides are joined by VS (or ranked by placement in FFA), and the villain's minions hang off the villain chip. The row shape then tells you the mode at a glance and the badge only confirms it. Every option below shows the same four real cases: 1v1, 2v2, free-for-all, co-op vs villain.
