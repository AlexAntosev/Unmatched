The menu is a panel, not a native <select> popup: surface #14141c, 1px border rgba(255,255,255,.1), radius 11px, shadow 0 18px 44px rgba(0,0,0,.6), 6px padding, 6px offset below the trigger.

Items are 32px rows, radius 8px, 500 12px Barlow. Selected = rgba(139,125,255,.16) + #c4bcff text and a check; hover = rgba(255,255,255,.06). Lists over ~8 entries get the filter field and scroll at 212px. Never inherit the browser's white popup.
Epic rating — filled vs empty
{{ e.label }}
Empty stars are the outline glyph bi-star at rgba(255,255,255,.16) — never a dimmed gold fill, which reads as "half lit" at row size. Filled = bi-star-fill gold. 11px in rows, 15px in detail panels, 17px when editable.
